THE CONSTELLATION · A LIFE IN MILESTONES

Six moments that became eternity

  1. 1931

    Born by the sea

    Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu — a childhood shaped by humility, faith and hard work.

  2. 1960

    Wings take shape

    Aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology. The sky becomes a blueprint.

  3. 1980

    India reaches orbit

    SLV-3 places Rohini into space — a young nation joins an elite cosmic club.

  4. 1998

    The shield of fire

    Pokhran-II and the Agni missiles. The world learns a new title — Missile Man of India.

  5. 2002

    The People’s President

    Rashtrapati Bhavan opens its doors to a billion dreams.

  6. 2015

    Immortal

    Falls mid-lecture at IIM Shillong — teaching, dreaming, burning till the very last breath.
